# Krishi Setu - Design Guidelines

## Vision

Krishi Setu should feel like a premium AI assistant rather than a government portal.

The UI should be clean, modern, minimal and confidence inspiring.

Target users are farmers, but judges are the first audience.

Avoid clutter.

Every screen should have one primary action.

---

# Design Language

Modern

Rounded cards

Soft shadows

Minimal gradients

Lots of white space

Large typography

Simple icons

Fast interactions

No information overload

# UX Rules

Keep every screen under one scroll.

No more than one primary CTA per screen.

Never overwhelm users with raw agricultural data.

Always convert data into simple insights.

Examples

Instead of

NDVI = 0.71

Show

Vegetation Health
Excellent

Instead of

Soil Moisture = 18%

Show

Water Stress
Low

Instead of

Groundwater Available

Show

Irrigation Backup
Available

The app should explain outcomes, not display datasets.
